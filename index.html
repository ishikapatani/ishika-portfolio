✦ The Ishika Fact

About Me

✦ that's me

Hi, I'm Ishika. At 21, my life is a mix of scripts and storyboards. I'm a writer, director, and creative strategist who's obsessed with the "why" behind a story. Whether I'm drafting a poem, filming a short, or building a brand from scratch, I'm always hunting for that one out-of-the-box spark that makes people actually feel something.

I've always been a kid who loved learning everything. My mother sent me to almost every co-curricular class alongside academics, and I loved it. Dance (more than 5 styles!), painting, poster making, rangoli, calligraphy and more; I tried it all. In that process I figured out something most textbooks never tell you: the best skills are the ones that aren't listed in your syllabus.

✦ Milestone & Experience

LIT × Scapia

Five videos. One challenge. An IPL match we'll never forget.

🏆 Challenge Winner

LIT School × Scapia

LIT School threw us a challenge with Scapia (the travel fintech brand) and we said "bet." The brief? Create promotional content for the Game Super Shot launched by Scapia on their website. Our team of three went all in.

We brainstormed, scripted, shot, and delivered five promotional videos that hit different. Each one was built to capture the energy of modern travel, cricket, and the internet-brained audience Scapia speaks to.

We won the whole thing. And the reward? The Scapia team took us to watch the RCB vs GT IPL match live at Chinnaswamy Stadium. Walking into that stadium, feeling the roar of the crowd, and soaking in the experience with the Scapia crew was absolutely unreal.

Honestly, this was one of those moments where creative work turned into a core memory. From cracking ideas in a classroom to standing in a packed stadium. Not a bad trade.
